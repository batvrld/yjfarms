Our Journey

Rooted in Passion,
Grown with Care.

Founded on a small family plot, Yesu Jeevikkirar Farms has evolved into a leading ecosystem for organic coconut cultivation. Our story is one of patience and respect for the natural cycles.

We believe that luxury lies in simplicity. By removing chemicals and industrial shortcuts, we allow the true character of the coconut to shine through in every harvest.

Three Decades of Excellence in Coconut Procurement & Supply.

Founded by Mr. C. Solomon, Yesu Jeevikkirar Coconut has been a cornerstone of the coconut industry in Tamil Nadu for over 30 years. What began as a local initiative has evolved into a robust procurement and supply network, built on the principles of transparency, quality, and direct-from-farm sourcing.

Managed today by our partners, Mr. Joseph and Mr. Samuvel, with strategic coordination by Mr. Arulanandam, we specialize in bridging the gap between the lush groves of Tamil Nadu and the global industrial market.

Our Core Advantage

We work directly at the farm gate. By managing our own sourcing network, we eliminate unnecessary middlemen, ensuring our industrial partners receive consistent, high-quality raw materials year-round.
